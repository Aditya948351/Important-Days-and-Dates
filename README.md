## 📌 About the App

**Important Days & Dates** is a meticulously designed Android application built to help you discover, track, and learn about significant historical events and global observances happening every day of the year. Whether you are a history enthusiast, a student preparing for competitive exams, or just curious about what makes today special, this app provides curated, categorized, and detailed information right at your fingertips.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Daily Insights:** Discover historical events, national holidays, and international observances for any day of the year.
- **Rich History & Details:** Dive deep into the background and significance of every important date.
- **Categorized Browsing:** Filter events by categories like History, Science, Holidays, and more.
- **Modern UI/UX:** Enjoy a sleek, intuitive, and visually appealing interface designed for seamless navigation.
- **Personalized Notes:** Add your own personal notes or reminders to specific dates directly within the app.
